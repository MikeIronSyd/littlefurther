Drawer lower area can show "Français" and a labelled "Mode sombre" toggle OFF, with ample spacing. Do not duplicate a second logo/name inside the drawer; the existing header branding remains visible.

Footer inside drawer "Maquette · Données d'exemple".
Use a restrained border/shadow separating drawer from scrim, no glassblur, no landscape multi-panel sheet, no phone frame. All French copy readable.
